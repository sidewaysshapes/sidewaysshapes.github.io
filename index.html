Sideways Shapes are a band from West Dorset
playing punk-grunge tinged rock tuned for the 21st century.
The majority of their songs are originals, but
look out for occasional classic cover versions in their live set.
Gigging since 2020, when they were just 9 years old,
they have played festivals, local events and open mic nights.
